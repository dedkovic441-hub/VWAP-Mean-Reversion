Signal rules
LONG: A candle closes below the lower VWAP band with RSI at/below oversold. A later candle closes back above the lower band to trigger.

SHORT: A candle closes above the upper VWAP band with RSI at/above overbought. A later candle closes back below the upper band to trigger.

Exit: Backtest exits at configured take-profit or stop-loss, or at the end of the sample.
